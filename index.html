Methodology

Calculation

Expected CHB cases = country-of-birth population × country-specific prevalence (%) / 100

Country estimates are calculated independently and summed within each MSOA.

Population and geographic coverage

The model describes included non-UK-born country-of-birth populations in England and Wales. It does not estimate total CHB burden in the whole resident population.

Rates

Cases per 1,000 included residents use the population of the selected country-of-birth categories as the denominator.

Local Authorities

MSOA 2021 geography is linked to April 2023 Local Authority geography using the supplied lookup.

Interpretation

Outputs are modelled prevalent cases, not observed or diagnosed cases and not estimates of undiagnosed infection.

Important limitations

  • Country-level prevalence may not equal prevalence among migrants living in England and Wales.
  • Age, duration of residence, vaccination, diagnosis and treatment status are not explicitly modelled.
  • No uncertainty intervals are currently presented.
  • Use estimates for population-level planning and further assessment, not to infer infection in an individual.
